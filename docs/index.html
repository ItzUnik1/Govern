GOVERN

Lead a nation. Make hard choices. Build a legacy.

A political simulation for Android. Become Prime Minister, run the economy, hold parliament together, win elections and transform your country over decades. Free, no ads, no account, and it works fully offline.

What you will do

📈 Run the economyGDP, inflation, jobs, debt, trade and seven sectors, all linked. Every tax cut and spending choice has a price.
🗳️ Win and hold powerCoalitions, confidence votes, bills, constituency elections and campaigns. Lose, and you lead the opposition.
🌐 Shape the worldTrade deals, alliances, loans, aid and sanctions with 13 nations that react to you.
🏗️ Build the nationMulti-year megaprojects and a research tree. Weak states suffer delays and overruns.
⚡ Survive crisesDozens of events with no obviously right answer, shaped by your country and your record.
🕰️ Play for decadesOne turn is one month. Build a political legacy across many terms.

Install in a minute

  1. Tap Download for Android and open the file.
  2. Allow Install unknown apps for your browser when asked.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unrecognised app, choose Install anyway. GOVERN is not on the Play Store yet.
  4. After that, the game updates itself from inside the app when you are online.
GOVERN is a game, not a forecast. Party names are real; their positions, vote shares and all leaders are in-game approximations.
